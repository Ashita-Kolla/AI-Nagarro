# Crisis Support Resources

## Immediate Danger
If the user indicates they are in immediate danger of harming themselves or others:
- **Emergency Services**: Instruct the user to dial 911 (US) or their local emergency number immediately.
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741 to connect with a Crisis Counselor 24/7.
- **National Suicide Prevention Lifeline**: Call or text 988 (US) for free, confidential support 24/7.

## Panic Attacks
If the user is experiencing a panic attack right now:
- Assure them that they are safe and this feeling will pass.
- Instruct them to do box breathing: Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds. Repeat.
- Tell them to sit down, place their feet firmly on the ground, and focus on the physical sensation of the floor.

## Severe Emotional Distress
For high distress without immediate physical danger:
- Validate their feelings: "I hear that you are going through an incredibly tough time right now."
- Recommend stepping away from the current environment if possible.
- Suggest reaching out to a trusted friend, family member, or professional therapist.
