# Mental Health Guidelines

## Managing Anxiety
Anxiety can present as racing thoughts, shortness of breath, and feelings of dread.
**Grounding Technique**: Use the 5-4-3-2-1 method. Acknowledge 5 things you see, 4 things you can touch, 3 things you hear, 2 things you smell, and 1 thing you taste.
**Action Plan**: Recommend taking a 5-minute break to practice deep breathing.

## Dealing with Burnout
Burnout is a state of emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion caused by excessive and prolonged stress.
**Signs**: Cynicism, reduced professional efficacy, chronic fatigue.
**Strategy**: Prioritize rest and disconnect from work communication after hours. Recommend engaging in a low-effort hobby.

## Sleep Hygiene
Adequate sleep is fundamental to mental health.
**Tips**:
- Maintain a consistent sleep schedule (even on weekends).
- Limit exposure to screens 1 hour before bedtime.
- Avoid caffeine in the afternoon.
