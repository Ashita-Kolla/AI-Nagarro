# Workplace Productivity

## Task Prioritization
When faced with a heavy workload, it is essential to prioritize tasks to prevent feeling overwhelmed.
**The Eisenhower Matrix**:
- Urgent & Important: Do these immediately.
- Important but Not Urgent: Schedule these.
- Urgent but Not Important: Delegate if possible.
- Neither: Eliminate.

## Meeting Fatigue (Zoom Fatigue)
Back-to-back meetings drain energy and leave no time for actual work.
**Solutions**:
- Block focus time on the calendar.
- Implement 50-minute or 25-minute meetings to allow short breaks in between.
- If a meeting has no agenda, ask the organizer if it can be an email.

## Deep Work
Deep work is the ability to focus without distraction on a cognitively demanding task.
**How to achieve it**:
- Turn off notifications for 90-minute blocks.
- Use noise-canceling headphones.
- Set a clear goal for the focus session.
