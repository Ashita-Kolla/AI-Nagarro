This document outlines the standard corporate policies applicable to all Nagarro employees. Compliance is mandatory.

1. Code of Conduct

Nagarro expects all employees to adhere to the highest standards of professional conduct. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Maintaining confidentiality of client and company data.
  • Treating all colleagues with respect and professionalism.
  • Avoiding any conflicts of interest in professional duties.

2. Leave Policy Summary

Employees are entitled to annual paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and casual leave as per their employment contract. All planned leaves must be applied for at least 2 weeks in advance via the HR Portal.

3. Information Security

The use of unauthorized external software on company devices is strictly prohibited. Employees must complete the mandatory infosec training annually.

For detailed sub-policies, please refer to the complete Knowledge Base on the internal portal.
